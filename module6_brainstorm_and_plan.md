## 2. Research: What is next?
Once a learner has built their capstone AI application, what do they need to know next?
- Evaluation & Evals (Measuring if the AI is actually good - LLM-as-a-judge).
- Continuous Learning (How to keep up with the fast-paced AI ecosystem).
- Deployment & CI/CD for AI (Model degradation, prompt drift).
- The Community (Sharing, open source, HuggingFace, GitHub).

## 3. Instructional Design Strategy (master-instructional-design)
Objectives:
1. Evaluate: Learners will be able to implement an evaluation framework for their prompts.
2. Adapt: Learners will define a strategy to stay current with AI trends.
3. Share: Learners will prepare their capstone for community presentation.

Lessons:
6.1 The Reality of Production (Prompt drift, edge cases, evaluation).
6.2 Staying Current (Curating signal from noise).
6.3 Final Assessment (The comprehensive course exam).
6.4 Graduation (Certificate, next steps).
